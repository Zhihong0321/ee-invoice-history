Risk Watch — What Management Must Note — exposure + the clause that protects you
HIGH

The Disbursement Gap

Between completion and the bank paying 90%, Eternalgy has fully spent material + labour but holds only 30%. This is the largest exposure window.

Shield: Irrevocable LOI (bank pays you directly) + Deemed Acceptance (3 days) + Retention of Title + Right of Retrieval after 45 days.
HIGH

Loan Shortfall (loan < 90%)

If the bank approves less than 90% of contract value, the funding gap falls on Eternalgy unless caught early.

Shield: Client MUST settle the shortfall in cash before any procurement or site work begins (Conditions Precedent).
MEDIUM

Authority Approval Delays

Bank may require SEDA / TNB meter change / ST approval before releasing funds. Delays beyond 30 days post-completion stall your 90%.

Shield: Client assumes responsibility; must arrange partial release or pay interim bridging amount directly to Eternalgy.
MEDIUM

Client Cancellation After Approval

Risk of a "change of mind" walk-away after you've committed materials.

Shield: Binding after bank approval. No refund except (a) Eternalgy delay >12 months, or (b) system non-functional >60 days uncured. Otherwise all deposits forfeited.
LOW

Loan Rejected by Bank

Application formally rejected — project doesn't proceed under financing.

Shield: Keep RM1,200 admin/engineering fee from the 10%; refund balance in 14 working days. Or convert to cash terms.
LOW

Client Cancels Before Approval

Client withdraws after LO but before works begin.

Shield: 10% deposit non-refundable; if 20% already paid, that is forfeited in full too.

Management takeaway: Never procure or break ground until all four Conditions Precedent are met — signed LO, 20% deposit received, irrevocable LOI in place, and any shortfall paid in cash. That single discipline closes most of the high-risk exposure.

Can the Client Cancel? — by stage (Clause 6)
BEFORE BANK APPROVAL
✔ Can cancel

Client may withdraw before bank approval / commencement — but at the cost of forfeited deposits.

  • 10% initial deposit not refunded
  • If 20% procurement deposit paid → also forfeited in full
AFTER BANK APPROVAL
✘ Cannot cancel

Project is confirmed and legally binding on both parties. Client has no right to cancel, terminate, or demand a refund.

  • All sums paid not refunded
  • Eternalgy may also recover extra losses & costs
AFTER INSTALLATION
✘ Cannot cancel

As long as the system works, the client may NOT cancel on the basis of:

  • Change of mind / personal preference
  • Financing inconvenience / refusal to proceed
⚖ After bank approval, the ONLY TWO lawful grounds to cancel (Clause 6.3)
CASE (a)

Extended Contractor Delay

Eternalgy delays completion by more than 12 months, and the delay is purely the Company's fault — excluding client, bank, TNB / ST, regulatory, force majeure or supply-chain causes.

CASE (b)

System Unable to Operate

System stays non-functional for more than 60 days after install & handover, and the Company failed to rectify it within that period after written notice.

Outside these two cases, the client has no right to cancel, terminate, or refund after approval. Both are within Eternalgy's control — don't let a project run past 12 months, and rectify any fault within 60 days of a written complaint.
